Who We Are

Built in Papua New Guinea.

Langa Engineering and Construction Limited is a registered engineering company in Papua New Guinea with experience in electrical engineering and construction.

The company focuses its operations within Papua New Guinea and provides a diverse range of engineering, electrical, construction, power and maintenance services delivered by experienced personnel.

Our onsite personnel are supported by a resident management team with extensive experience working in and around Papua New Guinea.

  • Electrical engineering and power systems
  • Solar and hybrid power solutions
  • HV/LV systems and transformer installations
  • Generator installation and maintenance
  • Construction and general maintenance
  • Communication and internet systems
